## Direction

The palette builds on Sohum's existing colors. The deliverable is a raster PNG concept on white, not an SVG or an outlined vector master.

## Initial generation prompt
Use case: logo-brand.
Create a new, exceptional professional logo for the real company "Sohum Systems", a software engineering, cloud, data and systems integration firm serving enterprise and federal clients. This is a final-quality identity concept designed with the restraint, optical judgment and craftsmanship of a highly experienced identity designer.

Deliver ONE beautifully resolved horizontal brand signature centered on a clean pure white landscape canvas, generous but not excessive clear space. Flat vector-style artwork with exceptionally crisp edges. Symbol at left, wordmark at right. The logo should occupy approximately 75 percent of canvas width. No mockups or presentation objects.

Design a distinctive compact geometric S monogram from two precisely balanced interlocking broad forms, whose intentional white negative space makes the S unmistakable. Use a coherent geometry, subtly softened exterior corners, deliberate diagonal terminals and generous open internal space that survives at favicon size. The symbol should feel stable, intelligent and forward-looking, not like a twisting ribbon, infinity loop, chain link or stock tech icon. Mostly deep ink navy #16233F, with one restrained warm orange #E8622A structural accent, approximately 20 percent of the symbol. No thin strokes, tiny details, gradients, shadows or simulated dimensionality. Aim for a memorable silhouette that would also work as a one-color stamp.

Typography is crucial: set the exact name "SOHUM" in a refined custom-looking contemporary sans-serif with confident medium/semibold weight, slightly wide proportions, meticulous optical kerning and understated tailored letterforms. Under it, set the exact word "SYSTEMS" in smaller navy uppercase, elegantly spaced, left aligned with SOHUM, with considered vertical spacing. No exaggerated futuristic lettering, stencil cuts, stretched letters or excessive tracking. The wordmark and symbol should have equal visual authority and aligned optical centers. The result should feel established and premium, suitable for serious engineering rather than playful consumer software.

Only text: "SOHUM" and "SYSTEMS", spelled exactly. No LLC, tagline, slogan, explanatory labels, hex values, watermarks, border, supporting illustrations, government seals or trademark symbols. No additional variations on the canvas. Prioritize originality, simplicity, proportional harmony and impeccable execution.

## Final refinement prompt
Refine the attached Sohum Systems logo into a clean final identity presentation. Preserve the exact S monogram silhouette, the wordmark letterforms, the text "SOHUM" and "SYSTEMS", and the horizontal arrangement. Make every colored shape strictly flat: one uniform solid deep navy #16233F and one uniform solid orange #E8622A. Eliminate all gradients, shading, texture, edge glow, or tiny irregularities. Preserve the crisp open gaps through the monogram and the careful alignment and spacing. This white background is essential; do not make the background transparent or dark. Give the complete logo balanced clear space all around, about 12% on the sides and comfortable equal top and bottom margins. Produce a pristine, high-resolution, professional brand master presentation, not a photograph or mockup. No added text, no extra variants, no shadows, no effects.
